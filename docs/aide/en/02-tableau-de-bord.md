# Dashboard

This is the **Today** page, the one you land on. At the top, **View site** and **＋ Import photos**. Then:

- **To do**: what needs your attention, **sorted by urgency**, each row with a button that leads to the screen that settles it. Urgency can be read without colour, by shape and by the order of the list:
  - **✕ in a square, urgent**: a **request for access or erasure** (with its deadline, one month after the request; “overdue” beyond that); a **refusal** whose linked photos are still published (**Apply the refusal**);
  - **! in a triangle, soon**: a person who **refused** while published photos from their outing are neither linked nor excluded (check whether they appear in them); **new photographed people**; **failed Instagram posts** (blocked by a consent or rejected by Instagram); **Instagram paused**;
  - **hollow circle, when you have time**: **photos to finish**, published photos **without a town** (missing from the world map), **titles without a translation** into English or German, **proofs of prior existence** pending after an error.

  Urgent items are also counted in the sidebar, next to **Today** (✕ and their number). The “without a town” and “without a translation” links open the filtered photo library; the filter chip, with ✕, removes it.

- **To finish**: drafts and photos that still have their provisional title, each with its direct action: **Publish**, or **Add title** (opens the details). **Finish everything on one screen** opens the photo library filtered on “To finish”.
- **The site right now**: the opening slideshow photos, in order, and the site theme. **edit** leads to [Public site](reglages#photos-de-louverture).
- **Figures**: today's visitors, published photos, drafts, series, photographers; a click opens the matching page.
- **Photo of the moment**: the photo most often opened full size over 30 days, with its number of openings. **Statistics →** opens the [Statistics](statistiques). This box only appears if photos have been viewed.
- **Pace**: the number of photos imported each month, over a year.
- **Recent changes**: the five most recently changed photos.
- **Proofs of prior existence**: number of timestamped photos, and those pending after an error, with **Retry now**. See [Proofs of prior existence](preuves).
- **Your account**: the name of the signed-in account, its roles, and the photographer it is linked to.
  - **Account not linked:** an administrator links it to its photographer in [Accounts and roles](reglages#comptes-et-rôles).
  - **Account linked:** your imports are attributed to you by default.

**“Updating images” banner**: it appears after a change of watermark, EXIF, author name… The counter moves on by itself; see [Watermark and EXIF](filigrane-exif#mise-à-jour-des-images).
