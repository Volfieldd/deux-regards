# Statistics

The **Statistics** page (sidebar) measures traffic on the public site, **anonymously**: no cookie, no outside service, no IP address kept.

## What the page shows

Choose the period: **30 days**, **3 months** or **12 months** (by month).

- **Visitors**: the distinct visitors of each day, added up over the period. One person who comes back on three days counts three times.
- **Page views**: home, series, perspectives, about, rights, and opened photos.
- **Photos opened full size**: each time a photo is opened in the viewer (pinch zoom is not counted).
- **On phones**: the share of visitors coming from a phone or tablet.
- The trend (“+12 %”) compares with the previous period of the same length.
- **Traffic**: bars for page views, a line for visitors; hover over a bar for details, or open **View the data** for the table.
- **Most viewed photos**, **Pages**, **Sources**: the name of the site visitors come from (instagram.com, google.fr…) or “Direct”.
- **Countries**: a world map with one bubble per country (its area follows the number of visitors; on hover, “France · 124 visitors”), and the list of the top 10. The country is the one passed on by Cloudflare: the site does not work it out and keeps no address. Nothing more precise than the country. “Unknown country”: Cloudflare did not give it. Countries are counted from the October 2026 update onwards.

## Elsewhere in the admin

- **Photo library**, a photo’s inspector: its views over 30 days, its rank, a small line chart.
- **A photo’s page**: the same “Audience” section.
- **Today**: today’s visitors and **the photo of the moment** (the most opened over 30 days).

## What is not counted

- **Your own visits** while you are signed in to the admin.
- **Robots**: search engines, link previews from social networks and messaging apps, monitoring tools.
- **Visitors who refuse tracking**: the “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” setting of their browser.
- Pages not found and browser prefetches.

## Privacy

- A visitor is recognised **for the day only**, by a fingerprint computed from their address and browser, with a key drawn at random each day. This fingerprint stays in memory and is forgotten at midnight: it is impossible to follow someone from one day to the next.
- The database only holds **daily counters**, deleted automatically after **25 months**.
- This is the framework within which the CNIL exempts audience measurement from consent: **no cookie banner** is needed. The public “Usage rights” page informs visitors of this.

> After a server restart during the day, visitors who already came that day may be counted a second time.
