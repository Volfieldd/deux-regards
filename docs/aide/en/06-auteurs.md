# Photographers

One record per photographer.

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Name** | shown on the site (“two perspectives” filter, captions, About) |
| **Address** | `/auteur/<adresse>` (existing records only) |
| **Short description** | their way of seeing, one phrase under their name (“mornings, quays, rivers”), 160 characters at most, on one line. In v2 of the site, the home page shows it after their number of photos, under “Two perspectives”. Optional English and German versions (empty: the French one). |
| **Biography** | shown on the “About” page; optional English and German versions, folded underneath (empty: the French biography) |
| **Instagram post signature** | added below the caption of Instagram posts of their photos that have a caption; can be removed photo by photo (see [Instagram](instagram#la-légende)) |
| **Consent card letter** | the letter on consent cards (J-0142); empty: the first letter of the address. It is saved with the record: changing the address afterwards no longer changes the letter (printed cards and QR codes stay valid). One letter per photographer: a letter already taken, typed or not, is refused (see [Encounters](rencontres)). |
| **In the field** | link to their consent cards and the QR code for their clothing (see [Encounters](rencontres#sur-le-terrain)) |

- **Save**: below each record.
- **Add a photographer**: the “New photographer” form, at the bottom.
- **Delete**: open **Delete**, then confirm. This is not possible while the photographer still has photos, encounters (an administrator moves them to another photographer from their record, keeping their reference), trips of their own or a linked account; the record says what is still holding them.
- **Renaming** a photographer updates their images if their name appears in the watermark or in the metadata.

## Linking an account to a photographer

The account that signs in and the photographer are linked in [Accounts and roles](reglages#comptes-et-rôles), reserved for administrators: choose the photographer in **Linked photographer**, then **Save**. Once linked, their imports are credited to them by default, and the dashboard says “This account is linked to the photographer …”. A photographer can only be linked to one account.
