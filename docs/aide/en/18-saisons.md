# Exhibitions

**Exhibitions** is the workspace for Nicephos photographic seasons. An exhibition combines an artistic intention, dates and an ordered display. Photographs keep their series and publication status in the photo library.

This chapter covers exhibition preparation, voting proposals and their approvals, retrospectives and permanent collections. Public presentations v2 and v3 show these contents according to their dates and publication decisions. The **Exhibitions** section must be published in v2 or v3 for visitors to access it.

The list shows the exhibitions you can access, newest first. The **State** filter distinguishes drafts, scheduled, open, closed and withdrawn exhibitions. A photographer prepares their own exhibitions; the reviewer can read them. The administrator prepares, schedules, approves opening and withdraws exhibitions. Choosing what is featured on the home page is reserved for the administrator.

## Prepare an exhibition

Create a draft with its French title, then open its record. Fields are grouped by language: **Français**, **English** and **Deutsch**.

- **Title** and **Artistic intention** introduce the exhibition’s theme.
- **Subtitle**, **Prologue** and **Conclusion** explain the start and end of the visit.
- **Cover** selects a photograph from the display; it must remain published and authorised.
- Exhibition texts do not replace the photographs’ general texts in the photo library.

A draft may be incomplete. To schedule or approve the opening, the title and artistic intention must be provided in all three languages. When an optional text is used, all three versions must also be completed. A missing translation is reported; French does not automatically replace it for this approval.

**Save changes** saves texts, dates and the display together. The page does not save automatically: save before changing the search, selection page or record.

## Choose and order photographs

**Add published photos** offers a paginated selection of photographs already published. Search by title, select the photographs you want, then use **Add selected photos and save**. They are added at the end of the display. Each photograph appears only once.

To change the order, assign distinct positions to the photographs, then save. Positions become consecutive after saving. This works without dragging and without JavaScript, including on phones.

The **exhibition caption** describes a photograph in this context. It has French, English and German versions; if used, all three versions are required to approve the exhibition. It does not change the photograph’s general caption.

**Remove** takes the photograph out of this display. It remains in the photo library and its series. If the cover is removed from the display, choose another cover before approval.

A photograph that becomes unavailable is still flagged during preparation. Remove it from the display before scheduling or approving the opening; the workspace never republishes it automatically.

## Dates and approval

Opening and closing are entered to the minute, in the **UTC time zone displayed in the record**. The opening time is included; the exhibition is closed at the closing time. Closing must be later than opening. Entered times do not follow the phone’s or computer’s time zone.

Save your changes first. Each decision opens a summary: review the title, artistic intention, dates, time zone, photographs and their order before confirming.

| Action | Effect in the workspace |
|---|---|
| **Schedule…** | Approves the content and a future opening. Texts, dates and the display are then locked. |
| **Approve opening…** | Approves an exhibition whose opening time has been reached and whose closing time has not passed. |
| **Cancel scheduled opening…** | Before opening only, makes the draft editable again. |
| **Withdraw the season…** | Withdraws a scheduled or approved exhibition after a reason is selected and confirmed. Photographs are not deleted. |

Scheduling, opening and withdrawal are separate decisions from saving a draft. An approved exhibition cannot be silently edited; a scheduled opening may be cancelled before it begins to resume the draft. This workspace does not offer reopening after withdrawal.

## Public visit and home page

Before opening, a scheduled exhibition announces only its cover, title and UTC dates. Its other texts, captions and display photographs remain private until opening. Drafts and withdrawn exhibitions have no public page.

At the opening time, visitors can read the subtitle, artistic intention and prologue, and explore the ordered display. Each photograph opens with its exhibition caption. **Previous photograph**, **Next photograph** and **Finish the visit** lead to the conclusion. The same journey works on computers and phones without JavaScript.

Closing ends the exhibition. The full display remains available for **twelve UTC calendar months after closing**. At that deadline, the same address retains the retrospective; the exhibition’s photographs and cover are no longer shown on that page. Photographs may remain published elsewhere on the site under their own permissions.

The administrator explicitly chooses the exhibition featured on the home page. Its announcement, open visit or archive then replaces the usual opening; the photographers, series and gallery remain available below. No exhibition is selected automatically. A withdrawn, invalid or expired choice restores the usual opening. The feature does not appear in filtered views or private galleries.

## Propose photographs for voting

From the exhibition record, **Photos proposed for voting** opens the advisory selection. This selection is separate from the exhibition display and does not publish photographs in a permanent collection.

| Responsibility | Allowed action |
|---|---|
| Original photographer | Choose their own published and authorised photographs from the display of their exhibitions. The administrator can also prepare a list, including for a shared exhibition. |
| Reviewer | Approve the review of the photographs and their order in the displayed version. |
| Administrator | Approve that same version after review. |
| Publishing direction | Reorder exactly the photographs already proposed, without adding or removing photographs through this form. |

Select photographs, then **Save proposals**. Retained photographs keep their order; new ones are appended. To change the order, an account with **Publishing direction** assigns a distinct position to each photograph and saves.

**Publishing direction** is a separate role, explicitly assigned in **Accounts and roles**. It grants reading access to exhibitions and permission to order their proposals. The Administrator role grants neither this responsibility nor proposal review. Roles can be explicitly combined on an account; no assignment happens automatically.

The list shows its version number and both approvals. The reviewer uses **Approve review**, then the administrator **Approve this voting list**. Each approval concerns the photographs and their displayed order. The latter action approves the selection; the exhibition dates still apply.

Any change to the list or its order cancels both approvals, including during an open exhibition. Proposals remain unavailable for voting until a new review and administrator approval. An outdated page is refused without overwriting the current list: reload it before continuing. This workflow is frozen when the exhibition closes or is withdrawn.

A photograph that is unpublished, removed from the display or no longer authorised disappears from the public selection. Its presence in a previously approved list does not restore it.

## Private preview

**View the private preview** shows saved texts and photographs in their order. The language selector lets you check all three versions. The preview is reserved for authorised team members; its address is not a public sharing link.

Check the full image, texts and captions. An unavailable photograph is marked in its place. The preview checks content before publication; it does not publish anything by itself.

## Errors and limitations

A message at the top and, where possible, next to the field explains a refusal. After a form error, submitted values are shown for correction; they are not reported as saved.

If someone saved another version in the meantime, the change is refused without overwriting their work. Reload the record, compare it with your input and apply the changes you want again.

An unauthorised photograph, a missing translation, a cover outside the display, repeated positions or invalid dates prevent approval. A refusal does not partially publish an exhibition. Voting proposals and retrospectives are managed in their dedicated spaces, reached from the exhibition record.

## Advisory voting and visitor rights

From an eligible photograph in an open exhibition, **Vote for this photograph** opens a form. At the first vote, the visitor reads the information and ticks their agreement to the cookie and processing of votes. The box starts unchecked. Without agreement, visitors remain free to browse and no voting cookie is set.

Visitors can vote once on each proposed photograph using this browser in this exhibition. Further photographs reuse that agreement. A duplicate shows the recorded vote without adding another. The cookie expires when the exhibition closes; changing browser or device or deleting the cookie may allow another vote. There is no account or identity check.

**My votes in this exhibition** shows this browser’s votes. Before closing, **Export my votes (JSON)** downloads a copy with the agreement date and information version. After confirmation, **Withdraw consent and cancel my votes** deletes these votes and their proof, removes the cookie and reduces the corresponding totals. Other browsers’ votes remain unchanged. Without the cookie, individual votes cannot be retrieved.

In **Photos proposed for voting**, authorised team members see **Advisory votes** per photograph. These totals are private; no score automatically publishes a photograph in the permanent collection. Proposals and their order remain subject to the approval process.

At closing in UTC or when the exhibition is withdrawn, identifiers and detailed votes are removed from the active database at the next cleanup (every minute, at startup and on visits). Only totals remain and exports no longer return individual votes. Existing backups follow their normal 14-day rotation. A photograph that is unpublished, unauthorised or removed from the proposals cannot receive further votes. Visitors can continue viewing the exhibition when voting is temporarily unavailable.

## Retrospectives and corrections

In the exhibition record, **Retrospectives and permanent exhibitions** opens a separate space. A photographer prepares retrospectives for their own exhibitions; the reviewer can read them. The administrator decides when to publish or withdraw them.

Create the draft with a French title, then complete the **Title** and **Account** in French, English and German. Prepare the draft before closing if needed, including while the exhibition itself is still a draft. Publishing requires an approved, visible and closed exhibition, with all three languages complete.

The retrospective may select **zero, one or several photographs** from the original display. Select the chosen photographs and assign distinct positions before saving. A photograph must remain published and authorised; selecting it does not republish it.

**Publish…** opens a confirmation. Once published, the title, account and selection cannot change. Use **Prepare a correction** to add a clarification or correct a mistake: this creates a new retrospective linked to the previous one and subject to the same approval. The public page retains both accounts and their links. A further correction may clarify a published corrective retrospective.

**Withdraw…** hides a retrospective without rewriting it. Corrections no longer display links to a withdrawn retrospective. A withdrawn retrospective cannot become the new origin of a collection.

## Permanent collections

From a published retrospective, **Prepare a collection** creates an independent continuation, presented as a new exhibition. Its **Title**, **Artistic intention**, optional texts, **Cover**, **Exhibition captions** and order belong to the collection. Photographs keep their original series.

Choose photographs from that retrospective’s selection. A published collection requires at least one photograph, an explicitly chosen cover from its display, and the title and artistic intention in all three languages. Complete all three versions of each optional text used, including captions. **Save the collection** saves the texts, cover, captions and order together; save before leaving the page.

After confirming **Publish…**, the presentation and display are locked. **Withdraw…** removes the collection from the public site. Each photograph’s current permissions still apply: unpublishing or a missing agreement removes it from the visit, even when it belonged to the published collection. An unavailable cover is not automatically replaced.

On the v2 or v3 public site, reach **Permanent collections** from exhibitions, their home page invitation and the site footer when a collection is published. Each collection has its own entrance, photographic visit, conclusion and links to its original exhibition and retrospective. After the exhibition’s twelve-month archive, its retrospectives and links remain without displaying its former photographs again. The collection keeps its own visit without a closing date. Withdrawing its origin hides that lineage; the collection remains accessible until its own withdrawal.
